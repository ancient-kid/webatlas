Financing climate adaptation in cities

Adaptation finance in cities comes from four main sources: local taxes and fees, national transfers, international climate funds and private capital. Private capital remains small because adaptation projects rarely generate cash flows that investors can capture directly.

Blended finance, in which public money absorbs first losses, is the most promising route to attract institutional investors. Green and resilience bonds are growing but remain a small share of total municipal borrowing.
