Rotterdam water squares

The Benthemplein water square stores up to 1.7 million litres of rainwater in three sunken basins. In dry weather the basins are used for basketball, skating and outdoor theatre. The square was built for about four million euros and financed jointly by the municipality and the regional water authority.

Evaluations found that residents value the dual use, although maintenance costs are higher than for a conventional underground tank. The design has since been copied in Copenhagen and several Chinese sponge-city pilots.
